## Research Domains

### 1. Demand Forecasting

**Definition**: Predicting future demand for products or services

**Key Topics**:
- Time series analysis
- Causal forecasting
- Machine learning methods
- Forecast accuracy metrics
- Forecast value added (FVA)
- Collaborative forecasting
- New product forecasting

**Common Methods**:
- Statistical: ARIMA, Exponential Smoothing, Regression
- Machine Learning: Random Forest, XGBoost, Neural Networks
- Deep Learning: LSTM, GRU, Transformers, N-BEATS
- Ensemble: Averaging, Stacking, Boosting

**Performance Metrics**:
- MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error)
- RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error)
- MAE (Mean Absolute Error)
- Bias
- WMAPE (Weighted MAPE)

**Recent Research Directions**:
- Hierarchical forecasting
- Probabilistic forecasting
- Transfer learning across products
- Attention mechanisms
- External data integration

### 2. Inventory Management

**Definition**: Optimization of inventory levels to balance cost and service

**Key Topics**:
- Economic order quantity (EOQ)
- Safety stock optimization
- Reorder point (ROP) determination
- Multi-echelon inventory
- Inventory pooling
- Vendor-managed inventory (VMI)
- Dynamic inventory management

**Optimization Approaches**:
- Newsvendor model
- Base stock policy
- (s, S) policy
- Periodic review systems
- Continuous review systems
- Stochastic inventory models

**Performance Metrics**:
- Inventory turnover
- Days of supply
- Fill rate / Service level
- Stockout frequency
- Inventory carrying cost
- Working capital requirements

**Research Areas**:
- Real-time inventory optimization
- AI-driven replenishment
- Multi-objective optimization
- Sustainability considerations
- Omnichannel inventory

### 4. Transportation & Logistics

**Definition**: Movement and storage of goods

**Key Topics**:

**Optimization Problems**:

**Research Areas**:

### 6. Supply Chain Risk Management

- Operational risks (process, technology)
- External risks (natural disasters, geopolitics)
- Financial risks (currency, commodity prices)

**Risk Management Strategies**:
- Risk identification and mapping
- Risk assessment (probability × impact)
- Risk mitigation strategies
- Business continuity planning
- Resilience building
- Agility development

**Quantitative Methods**:
- Monte Carlo simulation
- Scenario analysis
- Value at Risk (VaR)
- Stress testing
- Network analysis
- Machine learning for risk prediction

**Research Directions**:
- Resilience metrics
- Digital twin for risk simulation
- AI-powered risk detection
- Blockchain for transparency
- Climate risk modeling
