## Handoff rule

When handing off to a specialist skill, carry forward:

- scoped decision statement;
- trigger and end condition;
- decision owner;
- evidence map;
- constraints and prohibited actions;
- stakes, reversibility, and consequences;
- readiness verdict;
- delegation model;
- success and stop criteria.

Do not let the specialist skill broaden the scope without explicit user agreement.
