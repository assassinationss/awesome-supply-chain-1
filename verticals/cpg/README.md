## Overview

The Consumer Packaged Goods (CPG) industry faces unique supply chain challenges including:
- High-volume, low-margin products
- Complex multi-tier distribution networks
- Demand volatility and seasonality
- Rapid product innovation cycles
- Promotional planning complexity
- Direct-to-consumer (DTC) channel growth

## Case Studies

### Network Optimization

**Scenario**: Multi-regional CPG company optimizing distribution network
- **Challenge**: 150+ distribution centers, increasing costs
- **Solution**: Network optimization analysis
- **Results**: 15-20% reduction in distribution costs

### Demand Planning

**Scenario**: CPG company improving promotional forecast accuracy
- **Challenge**: 40% MAPE on promotional SKUs
- **Solution**: ML-based ensemble forecasting
- **Results**: Improved accuracy to 25% MAPE

## Best Practices

1. **Regular Network Reviews**: Conduct network optimization every 6-12 months
2. **Control Tower Implementation**: Real-time visibility across supply chain
3. **Collaborative Planning**: CPFR with key retail partners
4. **Data Integration**: Consolidate data from multiple sources (POS, shipments, inventory)
5. **Agile Response**: Build flexibility to respond to demand shifts
6. **Sustainability Focus**: Integrate environmental goals into supply chain decisions
